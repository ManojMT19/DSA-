## Quick tip going forward

Greedy problems often don't have a "generic algorithm template" like heaps do (min-heap/max-heap pops) — the skill is **recognizing which greedy choice is provably safe** (usually provable via exchange argument or sorting intuition). When you solve each one, try to articulate **"why does the greedy choice work here, and why doesn't a smarter/lazier alternative beat it"** — that's exactly the kind of explanation interviewers probe for with greedy problems.
